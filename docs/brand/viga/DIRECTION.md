# VIGA — identity direction

VIGA is a working name for the global fieldwork operating system formerly explored as RUMAR/RUMARQ.

## Idea

A beam is not the work people notice; it is the structure that lets everything else work. The product plays the same role for technical teams: it holds requests, people, routes, customer context and follow-through together.

## Rules

- The master identity is monochrome: near-black `#101311` on bone `#F5F4EF`.
- Existing deep green, coral and mint remain product colours for actions, state and navigation—not decoration inside the logo.
- The symbol must work without the wordmark at 16 px, on an app icon, a van and workwear.
- No gradients, AI sparkles, bots, circuits, arrows or trade-specific tools.
- The mark is a structural joint made from three spans; it is not a literal letter V.

## Voice

- Product descriptor: `The fieldwork system.`
- Brand line: `The system behind the work.`
- Portuguese launch line: `O sistema por trás do trabalho.`

## Before adoption

The name is a strategic design candidate, not a completed legal clearance. Search EUIPO/WIPO and obtain professional trademark advice in the intended software and business-service classes before registration or a public launch.
