# RUMARQ — global rebrand

## Decision

RUMARQ is the recommended working name for international validation. It keeps
the recognisable RUMAR symbol and its idea of direction, while creating a more
distinctive search and app-store identity. This is a working brand until formal
EUIPO/WIPO and relevant national trademark clearance is complete.

The original `RUMAR` name should not be expanded globally without clearance:
an AI writing product already operates under that exact name and `Rumar GPS`
is present in the App Store.

## Product category

**AI field operations system**

Not a generic CRM, calendar, chatbot, or invoicing app. RUMARQ is the daily
operating layer between an incoming request and completed field work.

## Positioning

For independent tradespeople and small field teams who lose time between calls,
messages, addresses and jobs, RUMARQ turns every request into the next clear
action. Unlike complex field-service suites, it starts with the working day,
learns the professional's operating preferences and prepares the administrative
work for human approval.

## Brand promise

**Work moves forward.**

Portuguese launch line: **O trabalho avança.**

Supporting line: **From request to done, without the busywork.**

## Message hierarchy

1. Know what to do next.
2. Keep every request, customer, route and job connected.
3. Let the system prepare the repetitive work.
4. Stay in control of every action.

Do not lead with “AI”. Demonstrate saved coordination first. AI is the engine,
not the promise.

## Audiences

- Launch: solo electricians and plumbers in Portugal.
- Expansion: teams of 2–20 in electrical, plumbing, HVAC and maintenance.
- Later: other appointment-based field services with mobile teams.

Avoid claiming to serve every trade before workflows and vocabulary have been
validated in that trade and country.

## Naming system

- Company: Dongrie Labs
- Product: RUMARQ
- Category descriptor: Field operations system
- Intelligence layer: RUMARQ Assist
- Daily planning: Today
- Request capture: Intake
- Route planning: Route
- Customer-facing portal: RUMARQ Link

Do not give the AI a human name. It should feel like a reliable capability of
the system, not an autonomous employee pretending to be a person.

## Visual direction

“A grounded field-work system where the next action is always primary, controls
stay familiar, and a warm signal colour only appears when work needs attention.”

The existing R/route symbol is retained. Its two paths represent the field team
and the system converging on the next completed job. It must also work in one
colour for vehicles, uniforms, invoices and small app-store sizes.

### Core colours

| Role | Colour | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| System | `#132D30` | identity, navigation, high-trust surfaces |
| Action | `#F07C69` | primary action and attention, never errors |
| Flow | `#A9DCCE` | progress, intelligence and calm emphasis |
| Canvas | `#F7F5EE` | warm neutral application background |
| Success | `#2F8A68` | confirmed and completed states only |
| Error | `#C84D4D` | destructive and failed states only |

### Typography

Use the native system family in product interfaces. Use Inter for web marketing
and exported material where a consistent cross-platform face is required.
Numeric times, durations and prices should use tabular figures.

### Motion

Motion communicates work progressing: capture, organise, confirm, complete.
Routine feedback stays within 120–220 ms. Route or state transitions may use a
short directional movement, with a reduced-motion fade alternative.

## Global product requirements

- Internationalised copy from the start; Portuguese and English are first.
- Locale-aware dates, time, currency, phone numbers, addresses and tax fields.
- Country-specific messaging, maps, invoicing and privacy modules.
- Trade packs instead of hard-coded electrician/plumber terminology.
- Tenant-isolated memory and human approval for external actions.
- Offline-first mobile workflows and low-connectivity recovery.
- Region-aware data processing and documented retention controls.

## Rollout

1. Validate the promise and workflow with the Portuguese pilot under the current
   build.
2. Clear the working name legally and secure primary domains/social handles.
3. Rename identifiers, store listings, website, app copy and Hermes profile.
4. Launch Portuguese and English foundations before entering a second country.
5. Choose the second market from pilot evidence, integration feasibility and
   willingness to pay—not market size alone.
